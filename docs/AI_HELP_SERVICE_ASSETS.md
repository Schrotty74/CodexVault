# CodexVault AI help service logos

The first-start screen uses locally bundled, unmodified official service logos
only to identify the linked service.

- `ai-chatgpt-logo.jpg`: official ChatGPT app icon from OpenAI, obtained through
  Apple's App Store distribution.
- `ai-gemini-logo.svg`: official Gemini symbol from Google, obtained from
  `www.gstatic.com`.
- `ai-claude-logo.png`: official Claude symbol from Anthropic, obtained from
  `claude.ai`.

ChatGPT and OpenAI are trademarks of OpenAI. Gemini and Google are trademarks
of Google. Claude and Anthropic are trademarks of Anthropic. The logos are used
